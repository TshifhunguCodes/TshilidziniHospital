Registered Nurse – Emergency Department

Urgent
Emergency Department Full Time Closing: 15 Dec 2025

Provide emergency nursing care, triage patients, and work in a fast-paced environment handling critical cases. Experience in emergency care preferred.

Requirements:

  • Diploma/Degree in Nursing
  • Registration with SANC
  • 2+ years nursing experience
  • Emergency care certification advantageous

Responsibilities:

  • Triage and assess patients in emergency situations
  • Administer medications and treatments as prescribed
  • Monitor patient vital signs and condition changes
  • Collaborate with medical team on patient care plans
  • Maintain accurate patient records and documentation

Benefits:

  • Competitive government salary package
  • Medical aid and pension benefits
  • Continuous professional development opportunities
  • Shift allowances and overtime compensation

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to apply?

You'll need a comprehensive CV, certified copies of qualifications, ID document, SANC/HPCSA registration (if applicable), and any other relevant certificates.

How long does the recruitment process take?

The process typically takes 4-6 weeks from application closing date. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted within 2-3 weeks after the closing date.

Do you offer internships or community service?

Yes, we regularly host medical interns, community service practitioners, and nursing students. Check our vacancies for current opportunities.

What benefits do you offer?

We offer competitive government salaries, medical aid, pension fund, housing allowance, annual leave, sick leave, and professional development opportunities.
