Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my medical records?

You can request your medical records by visiting our records department with your ID and patient number. Allow 3-5 working days for processing.

Can I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule by calling our reception at least 24 hours before your appointment time.

What documents should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring your ID, medical aid card, referral letter (if any), and any previous medical records.

How early should I arrive for my appointment?

We recommend arriving 15-20 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to complete any necessary paperwork.
