Frequently Asked Questions

What are your visiting hours?

General visiting hours are Monday-Friday: 14:00–16:00 and Weekends: 13:00–15:00. Special arrangements can be made for critical care patients.

Do I need a referral to visit the hospital?

No referral is needed for emergency cases. For specialist consultations, referrals from clinics or other hospitals are preferred but not always mandatory.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, medical aid, and hospital plans. Payment arrangements can be discussed with our finance department.

Is there accommodation for family members?

While we don't have dedicated accommodation, our staff can provide information about nearby guesthouses and lodges.
